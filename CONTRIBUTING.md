# Contributing

This repository is **EchoLive's public home**: documentation, releases,
issues, and ideas. The source code is developed privately and is not
published here. That's a deliberate decision, not an oversight.

EchoLive is a preview: try it, and tell us what you find.

What we warmly welcome here:

- **Bug reports**: the issue form asks for the Output log (View > Output >
  EchoLive); it narrates the call and usually names the cause. Tell us whether
  you started the meeting or joined it, and on which system.
- **Ideas**: what should a meeting inside your editor do that it doesn't yet?
- **Doc fixes**: typos and unclear sentences in these files. PRs welcome.

Reports for the whole EchoTools suite (EchoVoice, EchoAvatar, EchoMemory and
EchoLive) are also read at
[github.com/PiGON-AI/echotools](https://github.com/PiGON-AI/echotools/issues).

Questions about setup? Read `AI-PARTNER.md` with your AI coding partner.
